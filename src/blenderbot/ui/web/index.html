🧭 Why Floaters Spawn in Camera Arcs & How Regularizers Penalize Them
1. Multi-View Stereo Batching (views_per_step = 2..4)
Single-view SGD can cheat by painting a semi-transparent Gaussian right in front of Camera A's lens. Rasterizing 2–4 wide-baseline stereo peers simultaneously makes that cheat billboard project as a huge error across Camera B on the very same backward pass.
2. Camera-Corridor Free-Space Penalty (cam_corridor_radius_m)
Because the physical camera walked through the solved trajectory, any Gaussian entering within radius r of the camera path receives a repulsion loss 0.25·relu(r − d) plus hard eviction on densify ticks.
3. SH0 Diffuse Lock, Scale & Anisotropy Caps (Avoiding Overfitting)
On sparse interior walkthroughs or dark rooms with SfM pose drift, high iteration counts (>10k) and view-dependent SH1/SH2 lobes overfit pose error into mid-air clouds and 1m+ wall billboards. Using SH0, 5k–7k steps, opa_reg ≥ 0.001, and max_anisotropy ≤ 6 forces crisp multi-view geometry.
